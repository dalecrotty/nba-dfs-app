Measurement how every slider mix would have done, pooled over nights

After the games (5pm Sydney, catch-up 8:30pm), for every settled $2 and $15 contest, 67 mixes are built the tested way (chalk teams at the first tip; bulk swaps before later tips in multi-game; captain ×1.5 in single game) and placed in the real field: all 66 mixes of DC/BM/RW in 10% steps, plus the even split 33/33/33, plus the fitted blend: the mix that was most accurate on that slate size's earlier slates (the even split until 3 of them). Accuracy = each mix's average miss in DraftStars points on the players every source carries; it settles far sooner than ROI. ROI = money back ÷ money in − 1. $15 contests decide; nothing is adopted under 40 slates of a size.
